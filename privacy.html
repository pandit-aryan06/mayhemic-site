Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Mayhemic ("we", "us") runs mayhemic.in. This page explains what information we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email and message, only when you send them to us. Forms are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to our email.
  • Chatbot: messages you type are sent to our AI assistant (running on Cloudflare Workers AI) to generate a reply. Please do not share passwords or sensitive personal details in the chat.
  • Analytics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-friendly. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites.

How we use it

Only to reply to your messages, improve the website, and keep it secure. We do not sell your data.

Cookies and local storage

We store a small value in your browser to remember your cookie-banner choice. Nothing else.

Third parties

Cloudflare (hosting, analytics, AI) and Web3Forms (contact form). They have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us to see or delete the information you sent us at any time.
