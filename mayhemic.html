What Mayhemic is

Mayhemic is a young company building software and automations for problems people still solve by hand: paper records, scattered spreadsheets, and the same phone calls every week.

We start by understanding the work, then build the simplest tool that removes the pain. No complicated training, no bloated features.

First focus: schools

Many private schools still run on fee cards, registers, and Excel files. Cards get lost, totals go wrong, and chasing late payments takes hours every week. That's where we're starting.

Student list from Excel

Bring existing data in. No retyping.

Fees and dues in one place

Record payments, print receipts, see who owes what.

Parent reminders

Reminders by SMS and email, so staff skip the phone calls.

An assistant that answers

Ask "Which students have overdue fees?" and get an instant answer.
