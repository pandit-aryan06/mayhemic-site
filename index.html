Quick answers.

Updated 18 October 2026.

What is Mayhemic?

Mayhemic is a software company from Lucknow, India, building SaaS and automation tools. Its first product is a paperless school platform that handles fees, attendance, homework, marks and notices.

What does the Mayhemic school platform do?

It gives the school office, teachers and parents separate logins. The office manages students and fees, teachers mark attendance and post homework and marks, and parents see fees, attendance, homework, marks and notices on their phone.

Who is the platform for?

Indian schools that still use paper registers, fee cards and circulars, from small private schools to larger ones. It does not depend on the board, so CBSE, ICSE and state board schools can use it.

Do parents and teachers need to install an app?

No. It works in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer, and there is nothing to download.

Is student data secure?

Passwords are stored hashed, a login locks for 15 minutes after 5 wrong attempts, and every school's data is kept separate. Teachers see only their own class, and parents see only their own children.

Does it send WhatsApp messages?

WhatsApp fee reminders, receipts and absent alerts are built in and coming soon. They switch on once WhatsApp Business approval is complete. Everything else works today.

How much does it cost?

Pricing has not been announced because the platform is in early access. Schools that join early help shape it. Write to mayhemic.9@gmail.com to talk about your school.

Can we import our existing student list?

Yes. Save your Excel sheet as a CSV file and upload it. Hundreds of students are added in seconds, and duplicates are skipped automatically.

How can my school get started?

Send a message with your school name, number of students and city, using the contact form or by email. Mayhemic will set up a login so you can try it with your own data.
