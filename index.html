Questions

Good to know.

Do parents and teachers need to install an app?

No. The platform works in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to download.

Is our data safe?

Passwords are stored hashed, a login locks for 15 minutes after 5 wrong tries, and each school's data is kept separate. A teacher sees only their class and a parent sees only their own children.

What about WhatsApp reminders?

They are built in and coming soon. Fee reminders, receipts and absent alerts switch on once WhatsApp Business approval is complete. Everything else works today.

What does it cost?

Pricing is not announced yet because we are in early access. Schools that join early help shape it. Write to us and we will talk.

Can we bring our existing student data?

Yes. Save your Excel sheet as a CSV file and upload it. Duplicates are skipped automatically.

How do we get started?

Send us a message below with your school name, number of students and city. We will set up a login so you can try it with your own data.
